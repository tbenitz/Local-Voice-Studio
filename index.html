FOR THE BEST CLONE

Reference recording tips

  • Use one speaker only.
  • Clean speech beats a long recording.
  • Avoid music, TV, reverb and other voices.
  • Normal conversational speech usually works best.
  • 5–15 seconds of clean speech is a strong target.
